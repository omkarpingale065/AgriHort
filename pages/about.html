About AgriHort Connect

Bridging the gap between technology and agriculture

A community engagement project by SPPU Computer Engineering students dedicated to empowering farmers through innovative technology solutions.

Our Mission

To create a comprehensive digital platform that connects farmers, agricultural experts, and students to promote sustainable horticulture practices and improve agricultural outcomes through technology and community engagement.

Our Vision

To become the leading community-driven platform for horticulture education and support, empowering farmers with knowledge, tools, and connections needed for successful and sustainable farming.

Community Engagement Project

CEP-241: Connecting academia with real-world agricultural challenges

Project Background

This platform is developed as part of the Community Engagement Project (CEP-241) for Second Year Computer Engineering students at Savitribai Phule Pune University. The project aims to establish a mutually beneficial relationship between the college and the agricultural community.

Key Objectives

  • Identify and analyze local community needs in horticulture
  • Design practical solutions using engineering principles
  • Foster empathy, teamwork, and problem-solving skills
  • Create positive impact on agricultural communities
  • Evaluate effectiveness through community feedback
